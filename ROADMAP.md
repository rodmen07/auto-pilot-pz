### Shipped 2026-07-24 to 2026-07-25 (revival: in-game bug hardening)

The project was revived on 2026-07-24 and the user ran an in-game smoke test that surfaced five issues plus a fast-forward spot check. These landed as targeted fixes on `main`, UNVERSIONED (the Workshop update is USER-ONLY; the version bump stopped being so on 2026-07-26, corrected here 2026-08-01); every non-test change carries "Needs in-game smoke test before Workshop update." Full per-change detail is in `CHANGELOG.md`'s `[Unreleased]` section and the AutoPilot backlog `## Bugs`:

- **#67 (`0b45024`) — sleep pain gate (HIGH, user-reported):** the fatigue→sleep branch in `AutoPilot_Needs.check()` was terminal, so a sore or in-pain character queued a sleep the engine refuses ("too much pain to sleep") and addressed no other need. `AutoPilot_Sleep.canSleepNow` now mirrors the engine gate (pain MOODLE ≥2 with fatigue ≤0.85, panic, nearby zombies, sleeping-tablet bypass); the branch is taken only when sleep will proceed, else it records a `fail_reason`, tries one pain remedy, and falls through to lower needs (also fixes the LOW telemetry-blindness bug 4).
- **#68 (`6003365`) — dead Unhappy relief clause (MED):** a 0-4 moodle was compared against a 0-100 threshold, so unhappiness relief never fired; revived with a non-vacuous regression test (the prior test asserted against the constant's own impossible value).
- **#69 (`2ba8d5a`) — rest-furniture redesign (MED, user-reported):** `doRest` now SITS on any furniture via the engine-correct walk-then-seat chain (`pathToSitOnFurniture` → `ISRestAction(…, useAnimations=true)`) and never sleeps; beds are valid rest furniture; all seating is equal priority (nearest wins). Fixes the "reads Resting while standing" and "sits on the ground with a bed in the room" reports.
- **#70 (`9d7bbcc`) — fast-forward cadence (MED, user-reported):** `AutoPilot_Main.onTick` advances its decision counter by `getGameTime():getMultiplier()` instead of a flat +1, so the mod evaluates at a constant rate per game-minute at any speed; a real `speed` telemetry field (schema v5) replaces reliance on the misleading `ff` flag.
- **#71 (`6f98119`) — QA, fast-forward clamp coverage (test-only):** first coverage of #70's zero-coverage `mult < 1` / non-number freeze guard; two behavior-difference assertions (0x paused, nil multiplier), proven non-vacuous by removing the clamp. No in-game smoke test required.

- **#76 — sleep comfort (MED, the sleeping half of bug 3):** `doSleep` picks the most COMFORTABLE bed in range instead of the nearest, ranking candidates through the engine's own `ISWorldObjectContextMenu.getBedQuality` (comfort first, distance second) and degrading to the old nearest-wins behaviour when no quality signal is available. Completes the user's 2026-07-24 rest/sleep furniture decision: resting treats all seating equally (PR #69), sleeping prioritises comfort.

- **#77 — mood relief during a rest hold, and the literacy gate that made reading impossible (MED, the boredom half of bug 5):** two independent causes of the user-reported moodle accumulation, either one alone enough to stop boredom relief. (a) `doRead` gated on a `Perks.Literacy` SKILL LEVEL; there is no Literacy skill in 42.19 (whole-install grep: zero hits), so it returned false on every call and reading had never fired in-game. It now reads the engine's real gate, the ILLITERATE TRAIT (`hasTrait(CharacterTrait.ILLITERATE)`, `ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.lua:549`), failing OPEN when the trait cannot be read. (b) the V5.4 rest hold in `check()` returns true without queueing, so every seated cycle stopped ABOVE the mood step while the sit-to-recover loop kept re-engaging the hold; the hold now runs the SEATED subset of relief first (eat, read a carried book — the engine sanctions reading mid-rest at `ISRestAction.lua:44`) and skips the two arms that would walk. The hold is not cleared, so endurance keeps recovering while the character reads.

- **mood-food choice, and the gate that decides whether food is spent at all (MED, a further slice of bug 5):** `preferTastyFood` is called only by the UNHAPPINESS arm but ranked candidates by `getBoredomChange()`, so relief for one moodle was chosen by another and a food that reduced boredom while RAISING unhappiness was a valid winner. It now ranks by `getUnhappyChange()` (verified live: the tooltip's good-flag at `ISInventoryPaneContextMenu.lua:2079-2082` and `ISReadABook.lua:72` both treat negative as relief) with boredom as the tie-break, and reuses this module's own `isFoodSafe` instead of a weaker inline check, so mood relief no longer eats frozen or raw-cookable food that the hunger path already refuses. `HAPPINESS_FOOD_PRIORITY`, defined and documented since Phase 3 but read by no code, is wired to the food arm at its shipped value, so default behaviour is unchanged and the two happiness levels are now separate knobs.

- **media relief, the third boredom arm (MED, a further slice of bug 5):** new `AutoPilot_Media.lua` (22nd module) lets the mod switch on and use a nearby television or radio, the relief source the user's report named and the mod had no surface for at all. The engine's broadcast-code path was verified live before the arm was written (`Events.OnDeviceText` → `ISRadioInteractions.playerInRange`, same floor and ±5 tiles on both axes → `Interactions.BOR` → `stats:add(CharacterStat.BOREDOM, …)`, with 3729 `BOR-1` lines in `media/radio/RadioData.xml`), so this is not a repeat of the two arms that turned out dead in #77/#78. The arm claims a cycle only when it queues something, so a silent device can never park the character; while a device plays in range the mod stops walking outdoors, since `checkPlayer` refuses to run codes across an inside/outside boundary.

Still open in the backlog `## Bugs`: the rest of the broader-moodle-coverage half of bug 5 (Stress/Discomfort management and the telemetry `bored`/`stressed` fields, with a product scope question).
